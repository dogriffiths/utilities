Analysis of competing hypotheses

Did Oswald act alone?

Three hypotheses. Thirteen pieces of evidence. One matrix.

Evidence base through the 2025 document releases
The three hypotheses

What could explain November 22, 1963?

H1

Lone gunman

Oswald fired all the shots from the sixth floor of the Texas School Book Depository and acted on his own.

H2

Oswald plus conspirators

Oswald fired the shots, but others encouraged, assisted or directed him: organized crime, anti-Castro exiles, rogue intelligence elements or a foreign service.

H3

Other shooter

Oswald was not the shooter, or not the only one. At least one other gunman fired, for example from the grassy knoll, and Oswald was a patsy or a decoy.

Evidence base: Warren Commission (1964), House Select Committee on Assassinations (1979), National Academy of Sciences acoustic review (1982) and the 2025 releases.

H1 versus H2

Both score 2. What separates them?

Weighs against H2

  • Item 7: no co-conspirator, payment or direction found after the Warren, Church and HSCA inquiries or the 2025 releases
  • A conspiracy would be expected to leave some trace in 60 years of investigation, so I weight item 7 more heavily
  • Item 6: Oswald was hired on 16 October, before the motorcade route was fixed in mid-November
  • Hard to falsify: a well-hidden conspiracy would look the same as none

Keeps H2 alive

  • Accepts the same forensic evidence as H1
  • Oswald's Mexico City visit and pro-Castro activity
  • CIA and FBI knew more about Oswald than they admitted
  • Ruby killed Oswald two days later
Not refuted, but unsupported. Its evidence shows motive and intelligence failures, not direction by anyone else.
The extra weight on item 7 is a judgment call. It makes H2's practical score worse than H1's.

What the 2025 releases changed

What coverage reports

  • The March 2025 files add little on the assassination itself but reveal details of CIA plots
  • Oswald was on the CIA's radar months before the shooting
  • The documents don't settle whether Oswald acted alone
  • AP's summary: nothing points to a second gunman

Effect on the matrix

  • Item 11 is strengthened for H2
  • The ranking does not change

Limits

What would change the ranking

ACH ranks hypotheses by what they fail to explain. It doesn't prove the top one true.

The scores depend on how I weighted contested items such as 8, 9 and 11. A reader who weights the acoustic evidence or CIA foreknowledge more heavily would narrow the gap between H1 and H2, but would not rescue H3.

All ratings are my own judgment calls. The contested ones are labeled in the matrix.

Would narrow H1's lead over H2

  1. Weighting the acoustic evidence (item 9) more heavily
  2. Weighting CIA and FBI foreknowledge (item 11) more heavily
  3. An answer to whether a conspiracy could be completely concealed
  4. An answer to why intelligence agencies withheld what they knew about Oswald
