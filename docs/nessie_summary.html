The three hypotheses

What explains the sightings?

H1

An undiscovered animal

A breeding population of a large unrecognised creature lives in the loch, such as a plesiosaur-like reptile or another unknown species.

H2

Misidentification

Honest witnesses misread natural phenomena and known animals: boat wakes, standing waves, seiches, swimming deer, birds, seals, logs and unusually large eels.

H3

Fabrication and amplification

Deliberate hoaxes, press and commercial incentives, and a self-reinforcing legend generate most of the sightings.

Not fully exclusive in reality. Each is framed as the main driver of the body of sightings.

What H2 assumes

Witnesses honestly misread wakes, deer, seals and eels

What H1 needs

A large animal that leaves no DNA, bones or clear images

H3 has the opposite problem: it explains the famous hoaxes but not thousands of sincere reports. Discount the volume of sincere witnesses and H2 and H3 would tie. H1 stays last either way.

Limits

What would change the ranking

ACH ranks hypotheses by how little evidence contradicts them. It measures relative plausibility, not probability.

Best-fit synthesis: the hypotheses mix in practice. The most likely picture is H2 for the bulk of honest sightings, with H3 accounting for a few iconic hoaxes and the publicity around them.

Absence of eDNA is strong but not conclusive. A fringe idea in the literature, that Piccardi's tectonic gas released along the fault under the loch explains some sightings, is untested.

Would raise H1

  1. A physical specimen: a carcass, bone or tissue sample
  2. Unambiguous imagery or repeatable sonar contacts of a large animal
  3. An eDNA signal from a large vertebrate group that matches no known loch species
  4. Proof that wakes, seiches and deer can't account for the better-documented sightings
Evidence base: 2018 eDNA survey · Christian Spurling's 1994 confession · Adrian Shine and Alan McKenna on wakes, standing waves and seiches · 2025 Quest search · Loch Ness sightings register (2022–2024). E7 is my own inference and E10 is unverified background knowledge.
